205 Flicks MXS Shaders Mod
==========================

A free graphics mod for the Windows 64-bit version of MX Simulator.

Online play

The mod leaves mx.exe untouched. It has not yet been officially approved by RaceFactory Gaming (RF) or JLV. Wait for approval before using it online.

Installation

1. Close MX Simulator and extract the download.
2. Find the folder containing mx.exe. Back up any existing opengl32.dll.
3. Copy opengl32.dll and the entire 205 Flicks MXS Shaders Mod Presets folder beside mx.exe. Keep the preset folder name exactly as shown.
4. Launch MX Simulator and press F8 to open the graphics menu.

Menu controls

- Press F8 again, click X, or select Back to Game to close the menu.
- Click the tabs to switch sections. Click toggles and choices to change them, or drag sliders to adjust their values.
- Hover over a setting for an explanation.
- Drag the top of the menu to move it. Drag an edge or corner to resize it.
- Click Center to bring the menu back to the middle. Use the scrollbars to reach any controls clipped after resizing.

Presets

Export Preset and Load Preset open in the 205 Flicks MXS Shaders Mod Presets folder beside mx.exe. You can also browse to another folder from either dialog.

To restore the public graphics defaults, choose Load Preset and select Balanced.205shaderspreset. This preset keeps your current supersampling setting.
